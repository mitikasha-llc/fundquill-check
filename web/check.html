Verify a FundQuill document

Checks a FundQuill report, audit trail or assumption library: that it is unaltered, which device key signed it, and optionally that it describes your copy of the model. Everything runs in this page. Nothing is uploaded and no FundQuill engine runs; only SHA-256 and ECDSA P-256 are used.


    A signature names a device key, not a person: compare the fingerprint with the one the sender gives you through a channel you already trust. This check does not re-run the engine; the numbers are as produced, and the document records which engine build produced them. Source: github.com/mitikasha-llc/fundquill-check (MIT).
